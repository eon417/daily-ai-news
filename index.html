  • Works with Adam; noise scales need per-layer tuning

OPSFT: on-policy update direction explains generalization in LLM post-training

●●●●○ 4/5

On-policy RL such as GRPO keeps changing its update direction during training (cosine ~0.5), while SFT keeps a near-constant direction (~1.0). OPSFT masks SFT gradients to follow the sign vector from a short GRPO run. On Qwen3-8B with DeepMath it reaches 41.67% vs GRPO's 40.31% in under half the training time.

  • Challenges the 'SFT memorizes, RL generalizes' view
  • 50 GRPO steps + 100 OPSFT steps beat 700 SFT steps
  • Allows further training of post-trained models without capability loss
