Sharpening Tax in Post-Training (Meta)

●●●●○ 4/5

Meta researchers show that RL post-training raises pass@1 but narrows the range of solutions a model finds, which hurts test-time scaling on multi-turn agentic tool-use tasks. They tested 14 base/post-trained model pairs across four model families. They propose posterior-tempered group sampling (PTGS), which sets the sampling temperature per prompt based on estimated difficulty.

  • PTGS improves pass@1 and repeated-sampling solves

Hierarchical Continuous Diffusion Language Models (HC-DLM)

●●●○○ 3/5

HC-DLM combines discrete token readout with a continuous latent trajectory in a single denoising process. Tokens decoded at each step guide the next latent update, fixing the broken token dependencies of discrete diffusion and the missing token-level guidance of continuous diffusion.

  • Beats discrete and continuous diffusion baselines at matched sizes
  • Gains on Sudoku and Countdown
  • Better generative perplexity on LM1B
