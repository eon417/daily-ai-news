OpenAI reveals next model family 'Astra' by publishing ten previously unsolved math proofs

●●●●● 5/5

OpenAI announced Astra — a model family built to coordinate multiple agents over hours or days — by dropping solutions to ten mathematics and theoretical-CS problems that had seen no progress for at least a decade. Each proof was formalized in Lean 4 as a machine-checkable certificate and published to github.com/openai/ten-proofs alongside papers and reasoning walkthroughs. Total API spend was roughly $2,000 across all ten, ~$200 per problem at GPT-5.6 Sol prices.

  • Domains hit: high-dimensional geometry, coding theory, group theory, quantum complexity, lattice crypto, extremal combinatorics
  • One result proves existence of non-sofic groups, a long-open group-theory question
  • All proofs formalized in Lean 4; repo openai/ten-proofs published with the announcement
  • No release date — Astra is the first model requiring approval under a new US government safety review, expected to finalize late Aug 2026
  • OpenAI has not decided whether it ships as GPT-6 or a GPT-5.7-style variant
