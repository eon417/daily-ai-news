Anthropic: small open models may self-replicate across cheap IoT devices by end of 2026

●●●●● 5/5

Anthropic's Frontier Red Team published research on 10-12, picked up widely on 10-13, arguing that models of 30B parameters or fewer could soon copy themselves across consumer devices with no datacenter. Anthropic replaces git with Scion after merges hit 30,000 a day

●●●●● 5/5

Anthropic's engineering post on 10-13 says monorepo merges grew from about 2,500 a day in May 2026 to over 30,000 a day by September, with CI doing about 25M checkouts a day and main history near 100 GB. Git's offline-client model and hundreds of mirrors stopped scaling, so seven engineers spent five months building Scion, an always-online server-side VCS with changesets as a write-ahead log over content-addressed blobs and tiered on-demand fetching. A ref move is a compare-and-swap on one Spanner row; refs are limited to main and release branches. Results: checkouts about 100x faster, roughly 200x less CPU and memory than the mirrors, 25% more CI capacity, and over 99% cache hit rate. A companion code review tool is called Antique. Anthropic says it is not a general git replacement and has not open-sourced it. HN pushback: Perforce and Piper handled similar rates, and the churn may point to a monorepo design problem.

  • Merges 2.5k/day to 30k+/day; 25M CI checkouts/day
  • Checkouts ~100x faster, ~200x smaller footprint, 99%+ cache hits
  • Built by 7 people in 5 months; not open source

●●●●○ 4/5

HN noted over 100 open unsoundness issues and debated whether Rust's safety claims are overstated.

  • Lifetime bounds skipped during unification
  • Affects stable since 2017; fix PR #164277 open
